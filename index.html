Privacy Policy - Free Calculator

Developer: Blue Acid Labs

Last updated: September 2026

Contact: blue.acid.labs@gmail.com

Blue Acid Labs operates the Free Calculator mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use Free Calculator, including the use of Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, and Google's User Messaging Platform.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Calculation and Utility Data

Free Calculator performs mathematical, scientific, financial, salary, tip, loan, date, and other calculator functions directly on your device.

Calculation inputs, results, preferences, and supported calculation history are processed locally within the App and its device storage.

We do not operate a FreeCalc account system or a FreeCalc backend for storing your calculation information, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your calculation data.

App Preferences

Settings such as language and other supported application preferences may be stored locally on your device so that the App can remember your selections.

Google Play Subscription Information

Free Calculator offers an optional Pro subscription for $1.59 USD per month through Google Play.

Subscription purchases, payment processing, recurring billing, and subscription management are handled by Google Play Billing.

Free Calculator checks the Google Play purchase status available to the App to determine whether an active Pro entitlement exists.

We do not collect or store your credit card number, debit card number, or Google Play payment credentials.

Your Pro subscription remains subject to the pricing and terms presented by Google Play at the time of purchase.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Free Calculator is used to:

  • Perform calculations and utility functions requested by you.
  • Store supported calculation history and preferences locally on your device.
  • Determine whether your Google Play Pro subscription is active.
  • Enable the ad-free Pro experience while an eligible subscription remains active.
  • Display advertisements to users of the Free tier.
  • Comply with applicable advertising privacy and consent requirements.

3. Free Tier Advertising

The Free version of Free Calculator displays advertisements using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC.

Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with advertising, which can include device or advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, interactions with advertisements, and other information permitted by your privacy choices and applicable law.

The exact information processed by Google may depend on your device, region, advertising settings, consent choices, and Google's services.

Free Calculator does not sell your calculation history or calculator inputs to advertisers.

Users with an active FreeCalc Pro subscription do not receive AdMob advertisements from the App while their eligible Pro entitlement remains active.

4. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Free Calculator integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Where required, such as for users in regions subject to applicable European advertising privacy requirements, Google may present a consent or privacy message before advertising requests are made.

Free Calculator follows the advertising eligibility and consent information returned through Google's User Messaging Platform.

Where available, Free Calculator also provides an Ad Privacy Choices option that allows users to access Google's privacy-options interface and review or change applicable advertising privacy choices.

Users who previously provided consent may use the available privacy choices mechanism to revisit or revoke applicable consent.

For additional information about how Google processes information when its services are used in partner apps, please see Google's privacy information:

Google — How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5. Third-Party Services

Free Calculator uses the following Google services:

Google Play Billing — Google LLC

Google Play processes subscription checkout, payment information, recurring billing, purchase confirmation, and subscription management.

Google AdMob — Google LLC

Google AdMob provides advertisements for users of the Free tier.

Google User Messaging Platform — Google LLC

Google UMP manages applicable advertising consent and privacy choices.

These third-party services process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Free Calculator does not require you to create a FreeCalc account.

Calculation information and supported preferences stored by Free Calculator remain locally on your device according to the App's functionality.

You can remove locally stored information by using available clearing functions within Free Calculator or by clearing Free Calculator's application storage through Android device settings.

Uninstalling Free Calculator also removes App-specific locally stored information according to Android's normal application-storage behavior.

Information independently processed by Google through Google Play, AdMob, or related Google services is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion practices.

7. Google Play Subscription Management and Cancellation

FreeCalc Pro subscriptions are managed through Google Play.

You may view, manage, or cancel your subscription through:

Google Play→Profile→Payments & subscriptions→Subscriptions

Cancellation is handled by Google Play. Cancelling a subscription normally prevents its next renewal while allowing eligible Pro benefits to remain available until the end of the already-paid subscription period.

After Google Play no longer reports an eligible Pro entitlement to Free Calculator, the App returns to the Free tier and advertisements may be displayed again.

8. Data Security

Calculation data handled locally by Free Calculator is stored within the application's device storage environment.

Google Play Billing and Google advertising services communicate using Google's platform services and their respective security mechanisms.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we take reasonable measures appropriate to Free Calculator's architecture to minimize unnecessary collection and processing of information.

9. Children's Privacy

Free Calculator is a general-purpose calculator and utility application.

The App does not require account registration or personal information to perform its calculator functions.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through a FreeCalc account or backend service.

Advertising shown through Google AdMob is subject to the audience settings, privacy configuration, applicable laws, and Google policies configured for the App.

10. International Users

Free Calculator may be made available in multiple countries.

Third-party services such as Google Play and Google AdMob may process information in countries other than the country where you reside, subject to their applicable privacy practices and legal safeguards.

Where required, advertising consent and privacy choices are provided through Google's User Messaging Platform.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Free Calculator, third-party services, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When the policy is updated, the Last updated date at the top of this page will be revised.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
